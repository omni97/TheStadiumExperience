Borussia-Park

In the summer of 2025, we completed the full LED lighting upgrade of Borussia Mönchengladbach’s stadium and nine-pitch training complex. The stadium now features UEFA Level A ELITE LED floodlighting (>2,000 lux), 128 dynamic RGB luminaires and a DMX entertainment lighting system, delivering world-class lighting quality and spectacular matchday experiences.

DMX entertainment system and light show

The project was completed just in time for Borussia Mönchengladbach’s 125th anniversary. To celebrate the occasion, we designed and programmed a spectacular post-match light show following the game against Valencia CF, creating an unforgettable visual experience for thousands of supporters.

Our scope of work

  • Lighting design and infrastructure design
  • Installation of power supply and DMX control cabling
  • Installation of LED stadium lighting and RGB lighting
  • Lighting measurements for Bundesliga and UEFA
  • Programming of lighting scenes and light shows
  • Installation of LED floodlighting for 10 training pitches

ADO Den Haag

ADO Den Haag has taken an important step towards a more sustainable future by upgrading its stadium with energy-efficient LED floodlighting. The new installation features 72 high-performance LED luminaires, delivering excellent light quality while significantly reducing energy consumption.

The Netherlands

Go Ahead Eagles

Go Ahead Eagles unexpectedly qualified for the play-offs for European football, and after their win against FC Utrecht, European football was on the agenda at De Adelaarshorst. The stadium had to be upgraded quickly: the pitch was replaced, safe standing sections were installed, and new stadium lighting was added in accordance with UEFA standards.

The club chose to partner with us. Important factors such as light intensity, uniformity and spill light were taken into account, together with a plan to limit light nuisance for the surrounding residential area. As a result, the impact on the neighbourhood is five times smaller, despite almost twice as much light in the stadium.

130 LED luminaires and 2,000 lux illuminate De Adelaarshorst

In just a week and a half, 130 LED luminaires were installed, just in time for the European home match against SK Brann. The pitch is now illuminated with 2,000 lux, and a lighting management system provides dimming options during Eredivisie matches and other events. The result is something to be proud of!

“It simply feels like home,” says our project manager Steven Smals.

The Netherlands

FC Utrecht

Since July 2023, Stadion Galgenwaard has also been equipped with sustainable LED lighting. By switching from gas-discharge lighting to LED lighting, FC Utrecht achieves a 55% saving on its energy bill. Thanks to the installation of this sustainable LED lighting, Stadion Galgenwaard now also meets the UEFA Level B standard (> 1,400 lux).

In total, this project involved the installation of 120 sustainable LED luminaires and the removal of 192 conventional stadium lamps. For this project, we also upgraded the infrastructure, including the installation of kilometres of fibre-optic cables. These cables are required for the installation of the DMX entertainment system. This system is used to control the lighting for spectacular effects. As a result, players and spectators can now enjoy an impressive light show.

Innovative and sustainable

“In addition to the fact that this innovative LED lighting contributes to lower energy consumption, replacing the lighting also provides much better visibility for both spectators and players,” says FC Utrecht’s Operational & Financial Director Edo Keuning.

The Netherlands

GelreDome Stadium

As the largest theatre in the Netherlands, GelreDome hosts an impressive mix of top-level football matches, concerts and large-scale events. To support its demanding schedule, the stadium has recently undergone a complete upgrade of its lighting system.

Our team replaced the existing stadium lighting with a new, sustainable LED solution, delivering higher performance, greater energy efficiency and enhanced flexibility for both sports and entertainment events. The project was executed under a tight timeline, with preparations taking place alongside rehearsals for various artists and the possibility of a play-off match for football club Vitesse Arnhem.

The solution

In addition to the new LED floodlighting, the stadium is equipped with a DMX entertainment lighting system, enabling spectacular light shows and dynamic effects during concerts and events. The first visitors to experience the new lighting were not football fans, but the audience of Suzan & Freek. The popular Dutch duo performed ten concerts in a row at GelreDome, welcoming a total of 400,000 visitors.

The new lighting system combines atmosphere, experience and safety. With close to 1,000 lux on the playing surface, it meets the requirements for professional football while also creating the perfect setting for world-class entertainment.

Germany

Stadion am Zoo

Wuppertaler SV has upgraded its stadium with a new, energy-efficient LED lighting system, enhancing both performance and sustainability. The stadium is home to some of Europe’s most unique floodlight towers: four tiltable masts that are now equipped with 124 high-performance LED luminaires.

The new installation delivers excellent light quality while significantly reducing energy consumption. With this upgrade, the stadium meets the lighting requirements for both the Bundesliga and UEFA Level B competitions.

RGB and DMX

To further enhance the matchday experience, we also installed RGB architectural lighting on the grandstand roof, fully controlled by a DMX system. This allows the club to create dynamic lighting effects for matches and events, combining professional sports lighting with an engaging atmosphere.

ÖVB-Arena Bremen

The ÖVB-Arena in Bremen has been upgraded with a modern LED lighting system, transforming one of Northern Germany’s most versatile event venues into a future-ready arena for sports and entertainment.

The solution

The installation features more than 100 LED floodlights and over 200 RGB luminaires, providing exceptional flexibility for Bundesliga sports, concerts and live productions. The system delivers high-quality TV lighting, dynamic light shows and intelligent DMX control for a wide range of events.

The LED floodlighting system has also received official FIBA approval, meeting the highest international standards for professional basketball arenas.

The Netherlands

Telstar 1963

Telstar 1963 NV had been looking for a suitable partner to make the club’s lighting more sustainable for some time, but at the end of March 2021 the ball really started rolling. For Telstar 1963 NV and for us, it is a great case that shows a strong portfolio is worth its weight in gold. Telstar, nicknamed the White Lions, has been actively working on sustainability for several years. The club has established its own foundation, Stichting The Green Lions, led by Michael van Praag and Steef Hammerstein, with the ambition of making the stadium completely energy-neutral.

The solution

After an orientation phase to find the right installer for the new lighting, three parties remained. Hammerstein: ‘At that point, you no longer look only at the lamps; you look for a party that fits Telstar’s DNA. Telstar is a special club: an outsider, but also distinctive and accessible. We were looking for a partner that would match that.’ That partner became us. At the end of May 2021, we started work at the stadium and replaced the 80 gas-discharge lamps with 56 LED luminaires.

The Netherlands

Baseball Stadium Neptunus Rotterdam

Baseball and softball club Neptunus Rotterdam has made its complex more sustainable and expanded it. In partnership with us, the first major step was taken with the installation of LED lighting. Not only the stadium, but also the surrounding pitches were upgraded with both new masts and LED lighting.

In addition to high-quality light, the new LED installation also offers the option of lighting effects through the installed DMX system. The luminaires comply with the new regulations of the Royal Netherlands Baseball and Softball Federation. Partly as a result, international matches can be played in the stadium. In addition, a light-nuisance calculation was prepared in consultation with the Municipality of Rotterdam with regard to light nuisance for the surrounding area. In addition to the renewed installation in the stadium, the softball pitch has also been fitted with LED luminaires and a lighting management system. The second baseball field has been equipped with an LED installation at top-league level.

The installation of the new system results in significant savings on the club’s energy bill. In addition, the club can now train more and more often, and play matches all year round under energy-efficient lighting.

Go Ahead Eagles training pitches

Since 2019, Go Ahead Eagles has had its training complex at Sportpark Zuiderlaan in Twello. The sports park consists of several pitches where both training sessions and matches can take place. There is also a multifunctional sports hall. The accommodation, which is shared with amateur club SV Twello, was equipped with sustainable LED lighting in 2019. Partnership with FC Twente

We have joined forces with FC Twente in a three-year partnership. The goal: to help amateur clubs in the Twente region make their sports parks more sustainable. FC Twente was impressed by our way of working, and together we decided to explore the possibilities for a partnership.

This resulted in a three-year partnership. FC Twente has a covenant with 164 amateur clubs, and this partnership fits in perfectly with that. FC Twente acts as a connector between the sports clubs and us. Together, we have drawn up a plan to reach the clubs. For example, mailings will be sent and FC Twente will use its social media channels. In addition, the Sportverlichting name will be visible on the LED boards in the stadium and we will act as a match sponsor. We will also jointly organise several information evenings around matches at the stadium.
